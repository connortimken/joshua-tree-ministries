02   Outreach

How a morning goes.

Nobody hears a sermon on an empty stomach. So a meal comes first, then the person, then the hope. Swipe through.

1

Gather

The team meets up and loads food, water and supplies.

2

Walk

Small groups go where people actually are, not where it is convenient for us.

3

Feed

A meal, handed over with a name and a conversation, not dropped off.

4

Pray

If they want it. We ask, we listen, and we share the Word plainly.

5

Stay

We come back. Outreach is a relationship, not a one-time visit.
